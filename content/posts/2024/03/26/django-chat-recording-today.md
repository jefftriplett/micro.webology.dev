I was on the  [Django Chat podcast](https://djangochat.com) today, but since the episode will not come out for a few more months, I am sharing a few links for upcoming events and deadlines. 

[DjangoCon US CFP](https://2024.djangocon.us/speaking/) is open through April 24th. I plan to publish my list of ideas soon. If you contact me, I have a gist to share if you would like an advanced preview. 

[DEFNA](https://www.defna.org) is seeking a new [director/treasurer](https://www.defna.org/announcements/2024/03/26/defna-board-member-recruitment/) through April 9th. Our board seats only become available every few years, so this is an excellent opportunity to contribute to a non-profit and to help the Django community. 

[Djangonaut Space](https://djangonaut.space) [2024 Session 2](https://djangonaut.space/sessions/) applications open on April 15th and close on May 13th.
